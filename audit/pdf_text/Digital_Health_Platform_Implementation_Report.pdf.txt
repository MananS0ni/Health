Part 3: Advanced Workflows & Week-1 Deliverables
1. Lab Batch Ingestion (CSV Upload — 'Zero Extra Work for Labs'):
Diagnostics centers can upload raw batch CSV files containing multi-patient lab tests. The backend parses, auto-provisions
patient accounts, matches existing records, tags abnormal values, and routes reports into patient lockers in one click.
2. Granular Doctor Consent Protocol:
To protect patient privacy, doctors cannot view clinical history without explicit authorization. Doctors click 'Request Access',
generating a pending consent request. Patients receive an immediate alert on their dashboard to 'Approve (24h)' or 'Deny',
unlocking the doctor's chart dynamically.
3. Global Instant Search:
An omnipresent search bar in the app shell provides autocomplete across prescriptions, lab reports, test parameters, and
verified doctors with instant navigation.
4. Emergency Medical Card (Pure Text):
Text-based summary of critical patient information (Blood group, allergies, chronic conditions, and emergency contacts),
keeping the interface fast and reliable without QR-code or scanner bloat.
5. Cross-Portal Clinical Synchronization:
 Doctor Rx Sync: Prescriptions written in /api/doctor/prescriptions/ auto-generate patient locker medical records.
 Hospital Discharge Sync: Inpatient discharge notes in /api/hospital/admissions/{id}/discharge/ auto-inject discharge
summaries into patient health records.
Part 4: Live Email SMTP & Dev Resilience
 Live Gmail SMTP: Verified and active using EMAIL_HOST_USER=dhealth2026@gmail.com and port 587 (TLS). Sends real
verification codes directly to patient and provider inboxes.
 Development Resilience & Auto-fill: In DEBUG mode, OTP codes are logged to the console and returned with a 1-click
'Auto-fill' button on the OTP screen, guaranteeing testing is never interrupted by spam filters or delivery latency.
 SimpleJWT Token Pair: Access tokens valid for 7 days; refresh tokens valid for 30 days.

Part 6: Local Execution & SMTP Setup Guide
1. Launching the Backend:
Open PowerShell in d:\Health\backend:
.\venv\Scripts\Activate.ps1
python manage.py runserver
Backend live endpoint: http://127.0.0.1:8000/
2. Connecting Your Free Gmail SMTP:
Whenever your friend shares the email credentials, open d:\Health\backend\.env and configure:
EMAIL_HOST_USER=your_email@gmail.com
EMAIL_HOST_PASSWORD=xxxx xxxx xxxx xxxx (16-character Google App Password)
Django will instantly switch from console logging to live inbox delivery with zero code changes.
3. Launching the Frontend:
Open PowerShell in d:\Health\health_platform:
flutter run -d chrome (or your mobile emulator/device).
