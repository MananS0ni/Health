1. System Overview & Core Purpose
The Digital Health Record Platform is a unified healthcare management ecosystem designed to eliminate paper record loss,
fragmented medical histories, and slow report deliveries. Built with a modern Flutter Frontend and a high-performance
Python Django REST Backend, the platform seamlessly connects four distinct stakeholders: Patients, Doctors, Diagnostic
Labs, and Hospitals.

2. Detailed Patient Workflow (Step-by-Step)
When a patient opens the application, here is exactly how their journey works from start to finish:
Step 1: Simple Password-Less Authentication (Email / Mobile OTP)
 Login & Verification: The patient enters their email or phone number. The backend instantly generates a secure 6-digit
 One-Time Password (OTP) valid for 5 minutes.
 First-Time Registration: If it's a new user, they complete their basic profile (Full Name, Gender, Age/DOB, Blood Group,
 Allergies, Emergency Contact).
Step 2: Patient Dashboard & Quick Glance
 Health Vitals Tracker: Shows latest recorded metrics (Blood Pressure, Heart Rate, SpO2, Blood Sugar, BMI) with
 color-coded status chips (Normal, Elevated, High).
 Quick Actions: 1-tap buttons to show Emergency Card, view recent lab reports, book doctor appointments, or check active
 consent approvals.
Step 3: Digital Health Locker & Medical Records
 Centralized Vault: Every prescription written by a doctor, every lab report published by a lab, and every hospital discharge
 summary automatically lands here.
 What Patient Can Do: View records, filter by type (Prescription, Lab Report, Discharge Summary), search keywords,
 download PDF reports.
 What Patient Cannot Do: Alter or tamper with official diagnostic results published by verified labs or doctors (ensuring
 legal data integrity).
Step 4: Unified Medical Timeline
 Chronological Stream: Combines events across all clinics, diagnostic centers, and hospitals into a single chronological
 timeline (e.g. June 12: CBC Test at Lab Hub -> June 14: OPD Consultation at Patel Clinic -> July 01: Hospital Discharge at
City Hospital).

Step 5: Patient Privacy & Consent Control Center
 Strict Data Privacy: Patient health records are locked by default. Doctors cannot peek into historical medical records
 without patient permission.
 Approving Access: When a doctor requests record access, the patient receives an access request. When approved,
 access is granted for exactly 24 hours.
 1-Tap Instant Revocation: At any time, the patient can hit 'Revoke Access' to instantly cut off doctor access immediately.
 
Step 6: Family Account Management & Emergency Card
 Dependents Care: Patients can add family members (children, elderly parents, spouse) and manage their health records
 under one master account.
 Emergency Card: Designed for critical situations, presenting Blood Group, Known Allergies, Emergency Contacts, and a
 scannable Patient QR code.
3. Detailed Doctor Workflow (Step-by-Step)
Step 1: Doctor Login & OPD Dashboard
 Doctors log in with verified medical credentials (Registration Number, Specialization, Clinic/Hospital Affiliation).
 
 The doctor dashboard lists today's appointments, queue status, and pending patient access approvals.
 
Step 2: Patient Directory Search & Registration
 Instant Search: Doctors can search patients by Name, Phone Number, Email, or Patient Code (e.g., PAT-4726A2).
 
 On-the-Fly Registration: If a new walk-in patient visits, the doctor can register them directly. This automatically creates the
 patient account and pre-authorizes the doctor for 24 hours for immediate consultation.
Step 3: Consent-Gated Clinical Chart Access
 Unlocked Chart: If active consent exists (or for newly registered walk-ins), the doctor sees full medical history, past
 prescriptions, vitals trends, and lab reports.
 Locked Chart: If consent is missing or expired, historical records display a security lock. The doctor clicks 'Request
 Access', triggering a notification to the patient.
Step 4: Writing Digital Prescriptions & Automated Synchronization
 Doctor enters Clinical Diagnosis, Notes, and prescribes medicines (Medicine Name, Dosage e.g., 1-0-1, Duration e.g., 7
 Days).
 Auto-Sync Magic: As soon as the doctor clicks 'Save Prescription', the backend immediately generates a formal record
 and injects it into the patient's personal health locker in real time!
4. Detailed Diagnostic Lab Workflow (Step-by-Step)
Step 1: Order Queue Management
 Lab technicians log in to view pending diagnostic test orders placed by doctors or requested by patients.
 
Step 2: Verification & Publishing Results
 Lab staff input individual test parameters (e.g. Hemoglobin: 14.2 g/dL, Fasting Blood Sugar: 110 mg/dL).
 
 Abnormal flags (High/Low) are set automatically or manually. Once verified, clicking 'Publish Report' instantly transfers the
 official verified report into the patient's digital locker.

Step 3: High-Volume Batch CSV Processing
 Diagnostic centers processing hundreds of daily samples can upload a single CSV spreadsheet.
 
 The backend engine automatically matches each row to the patient email. If a patient is not in the database, it automatically
 creates their account and attaches their lab report seamlessly.
5. Detailed Hospital Care Workflow (Step-by-Step)
Step 1: Ward & Inpatient Bed Allocation
 Hospital administration tracks bed availability across General Wards, ICU, and Emergency Units.
 
 When admitting a patient, staff record Ward Name, Bed Number, Attending Doctor, and Initial Diagnosis.
 
Step 2: Patient Discharge & Summary Auto-Sync
 Upon recovery, hospital staff execute the discharge procedure and input clinical discharge summary notes.
 
 Hitting 'Discharge' frees up the hospital bed and automatically transmits a formatted Discharge Summary into the patient's
 digital locker.
6. How Everything Works Behind The Scenes (In Simple Words)
1. The User Interface (Frontend): Built with Flutter, delivering a fast, responsive experience across mobile phones and web
browsers. It never talks directly to the raw database; it communicates via secure API requests.
2. The Application Engine (Backend): Powered by Python Django REST Framework. It acts as the traffic controller,
verifying security tokens, checking permissions, validating inputs, and executing database queries.
3. Multi-Role Security Guard: Ensures that a Patient can only see their records, a Doctor can only see patients who granted
consent, a Lab can only publish test results, and a Hospital can only manage ward admissions.
4. Automated Synchronization Engine: Eliminates manual data entry between systems. When an action occurs in one
portal (e.g. Doctor writes prescription or Lab publishes test), background signals automatically update the Patient's personal
locker instantly.
