Comprehensive technical reference for Patient, Doctor, Diagnostic Lab, and Hospital workflows.
PROJECT: Digital Health Platform
AUTH METHOD: Free Email SMTP OTP
TECH STACK: Django / DRF + PostgreSQL
CONSTRAINTS: Zero ABHA / Zero Docker (Native venv)
1. Core Architectural Pillars
 Zero External ABHA / ABDM Dependency: The platform operates 100% independently as your private digital health record
cloud. There is no government API integration, no ABDM sandboxing, and no compliance delay.
 100% Free Email SMTP Authentication: No paid SMS gateways (Twilio, MSG91, Fast2SMS). The Django backend
dispatches secure, time-limited (5-min) 6-digit OTP codes straight to the user's email inbox using Gmail App Password or
custom SMTP.
 Direct Native Execution (No Docker): Backends runs natively with Python venv and standard local PostgreSQL/SQLite,
enabling fast debugging, simple deployment, and zero virtualization overhead.
 Frontend-First Alignment: The database models and endpoints directly mirror the state and models of the live Flutter
application.

3. Detailed Portal Breakdown & Capabilities
■ Patient Portal Capabilities
 Health Vitals Tracker: Records Blood Pressure (systolic/diastolic), Heart Rate, Fasting Sugar, and BMI with status tags.
 Medical Records Locker: Categorized repository for past prescriptions, consultation summaries, and medical history.
 Lab Reports Table: Diagnostic parameter viewing (e.g. HbA1c, Lipid panel) showing measured values and reference
ranges.
 Chronological Health Timeline: Vertical timeline showing all medical interactions, visits, and hospital admissions across
time.
 Family Health Profiles: Dedicated dependents management for children and elderly parents without requiring separate
phone accounts.
 Instant Emergency Card: Rapid one-tap emergency card displaying Blood Group, Allergies (e.g. Penicillin), and
Emergency Contacts.
■ Doctor Portal Capabilities
 Appointment Queue: Daily schedule showing booked patients, appointment time slots, and consultation statuses.
 EMR Directory Search: Live search across patient records by name, email, or system-generated Patient ID.
 Clinical Chart View: Full review of patient vitals trends, prior prescriptions, and diagnostic lab reports.
 Prominent Allergy Warnings: High-visibility warning chips alerting the clinician to severe drug allergies before
prescribing.
 Digital Prescription Generator: Fast medicine entry with dosage (e.g. 1-0-1), frequency, and duration (e.g. 14 Days).
 Instant Locker Sync: Saved diagnoses and prescriptions automatically appear in the patient's mobile app timeline.
■ Diagnostic Laboratory Portal
 Pending Test Order Queue: Filterable by discipline (Biochemistry, Endocrinology, Cardiology, Diabetology, Nephrology).
 Structured Parameter Entry: Input observed numerical values, units (mg/dL, cells/mcL), and biological reference
intervals.
 Report PDF Upload: Ability to attach certified lab report PDFs alongside structured parameters.
 Automated Publishing: Submitting test results immediately unlocks the report in the patient's personal locker.
■ Hospital Inpatient Care Portal
 Ward Bed Occupancy: Real-time bed occupancy stats across General Wards, ICU, Private, and Semi-Private rooms.
 Inpatient Admission Intake: Bed allocation, attending physician assignment, and primary admission diagnosis recording.
 Discharge Summary Flow: Formulate finalized discharge documentation with treatment summary, instructions, and
follow-up date.
4. Authentication Architecture: Free Email SMTP OTP
Instead of paid SMS gateways, authentication uses a standard email OTP mechanism powered by free Gmail SMTP (or
custom SMTP):
Step
Action
Backend Logic
1
User Enters Email
Frontend validates email format and sends request to /api/auth/request-otp/.
2
Generate 6-Digit OTP
Django generates a cryptographically random 6-digit code with a 5-minute expiration
timestamp.
3
Dispatch SMTP Email
Django sends HTML email directly to the user's inbox using free SMTP settings.
4
User Enters OTP
Flutter app accepts 6 digits and submits to /api/auth/verify-otp/.
5
Verify & Issue JWT
If code is valid, backend generates JWT access & refresh tokens.
6
Role-Based Redirection
Existing users immediately land on their role dashboard; new users complete role
registration.
